Jazztron

The right sound for the occasion.

Jazz standards, jazzy takes on familiar songs, and sophisticated pop, played live and matched to your room.

Jazztron is built around a jazz guitarist and a female vocalist. The music can stay intimate and understated for a cocktail hour or dinner, or grow into a fuller live ensemble for a reception or featured performance. The format and energy are set to fit the event, the venue, and the guests.

Formats

Three ways to book.

One act, scaled to the event. Choose the format that fits your room, your guest count, and the role music plays in the evening.

I

Solo Guitar

Elegant instrumental jazz guitar. Unobtrusive and polished, it sets the tone without competing with conversation.

Cocktail hours · Dinners · Receptions · Intimate gatherings
II

Guitar & Vocals

The core Jazztron experience. Jazz standards, jazzy pop arrangements, and holiday favorites, with a female vocalist leading.

Weddings · Corporate events · Restaurants · Seasonal parties
III

Expanded Ensemble

Additional musicians join for a fuller sound and a more substantial, featured performance.

Galas · Receptions · Fundraisers · Larger celebrations

Ensemble size and instrumentation are arranged for each event. Please inquire early so the right format can be confirmed for your date.

About

Led by Pat Hume.

A Louisville-based live entertainment project led by guitarist Pat Hume.

Pat is a professional musician and co-owner of Hidden Room Studios. Jazztron draws on jazz standards and contemporary popular music to create an adaptable musical experience for events, with a vocalist and additional musicians added as the occasion calls for them.
